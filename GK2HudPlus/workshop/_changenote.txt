0.7.0
- New: Folio at the alchemy table. Once the Folio is built, a cell with its book in the alchemy table window opens it; closing it brings the table back.
- New: available items first. When a station asks you to pick an item from your chests (alchemy, autopsy, embalming, garden, sermons, zombie equipment...), the usable items come first, added up across all chests and sorted for that station: runes in alchemy, skulls for organs, quality for seeds and sermons, mastery for tools. A button in the section switches to sorting by quantity; the chest lists can be hidden.
